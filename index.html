Universal Music Theory

A TypeScript library for music theory: harmony analysis, scales and modes, any tuning, rhythm, world music, set theory and notation export. One bundle, no dependencies. Everything on this page runs on it.

Tonal harmony

Progression analysis new

Roman numerals, functions, secondary dominants, borrowed chords, cadences and modulations. Type chord symbols or numerals.

TonicSubdominantDominantOther

Harmony.analyzeProgression · parseRomanProgression · formatChordSymbol

Identify notes new

Click keys or type notes. UMT names the chord with a score for each reading, describes the voicing, and lists the scales that hold the notes.

Harmony.detectChordsScored · analyzeVoicing · findScalesForNotes

Chord

One chord in every spelling style, its names, the scales that fit it and its substitutes in a key.

parseChordSymbol · formatChordSymbol · chordLongName · Harmony.getSuggestedScales · getSubstitutions · Harmony.getNegativeHarmony

Key explorer new

The chords of a key, what it can borrow from its parallel modes, its negative harmony, and the pivot chords to another key.

Scale.getDiatonicChords · Harmony.getBorrowedChords · Harmony.getNegativeHarmony · Harmony.findPivotChords · Harmony.getColtraneAxis

Key detection new

Correlates the notes of a melody, weighted by duration, with five key profiles. Write note:beats; the duration is optional.

KeyDetection.detect · KEY_PROFILES

Scales and tuning

Scales and modes

Notes, diatonic chords, brightness and parent scale. Over 80 scale patterns: try E phrygian dominant, C hirajoshi or D lydian augmented.

parseScaleSymbol · getDiatonicChords · getModalCharacteristics · getParentScale

Tunings

The same scale in equal temperament, 5-limit just intonation and Werckmeister III: how far each note moves, in cents.

EDO · JustIntonation · CentTuning · parseScaleSymbol(symbol, tuning)

Rhythm

Patterns

Euclidean rhythms spread k onsets as evenly as possible over n steps; many traditional patterns are one of them.

Polyrhythm.euclidean · CLAVE_PATTERNS · RhythmAnalysis.detectSyncopation

Rhythmic cycles new

Hindustani and Carnatic talas, flamenco compás and Balkan aksak meters, with the stroke of every pulse: a metronome that counts like the tradition does.

RHYTHMIC_CYCLES · cycleStrokes · cycleCounts · cycleTimeSignature
