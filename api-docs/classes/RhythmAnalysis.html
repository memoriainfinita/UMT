    StaticdetectHemiola

    • detectHemiola(pattern: number[], timeSignature: TimeSignature): boolean

      Detects hemiola: accents grouping six beats as 2+2+2 where the meter groups them 3+3 (two bars of a triple meter such as 3/4, or one bar of 6/8 or 6/4).

      Parameters

      • pattern: number[]

        Accent positions in beats from the start (0-indexed), over one span of six beats (two bars of 3, one bar of 6); later spans repeat modulo 6.

      • timeSignature: TimeSignature

        A meter of 3 or 6 beats; others return false.

      Returns boolean

      True when accents fall on beats 0, 2 and 4 of the span and not on beat 3.
